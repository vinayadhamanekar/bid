3The bridge

Reconcile finance cost to regulatory cost, then trace what feeds the bid.

Compare the sources, explain the differences, keep anything unresolved visible.

  • Match visits to claims using agreed fields (member, date, service, amount). Records that can't be matched are flagged.
  • Separate missing records, changed amounts and other differences, with the reason for each.
  • A list of every difference: the records behind it, how it was calculated, and whether it is explained yet.
  • Anything still unexplained is shown as its own line, never forced to zero
